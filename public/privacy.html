Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what BagVerse collects when you play, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to what the game needs: we don't sell your data and we don't run advertising trackers.

1. What we collect

  • Guest account: a random account ID created when you first play, used to save your progress and keep raffle entries to one per person.
  • Game data: your player name, chosen skin, level, XP, missions, items, gear and settings. Some is stored in your browser (local storage) and some on our servers.
  • Multiplayer: while you play, your name, level, skin, position, animation and chat messages are shared live with other players in the same world. Chat messages are relayed in real time and are not stored on our servers.
  • Leaderboard: your name, skin, level, mini-game scores and, if verified, your wallet address.
  • Wallets: if you connect a wallet, its public address, the message you signed and when it was verified. If your account holds BagVerse partner NFTs, we check which ones to unlock holder skins. We never receive your private keys or seed phrase.
  • Raffles: your entries and, if you win, the wallet the prize is sent to.
  • Advertising: for billboard bookings, the paying wallet, payment reference, booking dates, the image you upload, and the name you enter.
  • X sign-in (optional): if you sign in with X, we receive your X user ID, handle, display name and profile picture, and your email address if you allow it.
  • Technical data: our hosting and database providers process your IP address, browser and device type, and request logs to run and protect the Service. The game also stores your graphics setting and crash-recovery flags in your browser.

2. How we use it

  • to run the game, save your progress and let you play with others;
  • to verify wallets, run raffles fairly, deliver prizes and prevent duplicate or fraudulent entries;
  • to run the billboard service, show your ads and process refunds;
  • to keep the Service secure, enforce our Terms and moderate abuse;
  • to fix bugs and improve performance.

3. What's public

Your player name, level, skin and in-game position are visible to other players. Leaderboard entries are public. Blockchain transactions, including prize transfers and ad payments, are public by nature and can be seen by anyone on the relevant network.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run BagVerse, and only as needed:

  • Supabase (accounts, database, real-time multiplayer) and Vercel (website hosting and server functions);
  • Solana and Polygon network and data providers (for example RPC providers such as QuickNode or Helius, and Alchemy) to verify wallets, payments and NFT holdings;
  • X, when you choose to sign in with it;
  • Collector Crypt or Courtyard, to send you a prize you won;
  • authorities, if the law requires it or to protect players and the Service.

We don't sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and game data while your account is active, and raffle and advertising records for as long as needed to run draws, deliver prizes, handle refunds and meet legal obligations. Server logs are kept for a limited period by our hosting providers.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can play without connecting a wallet or signing in with X.
  • You can unlink your wallet in the game, and clear browser data to remove what's stored locally.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account removes your progress. Records on public blockchains can't be deleted by anyone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or Nigeria's NDPA, including to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Children

BagVerse is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Raffles, wallets and advertising are for adults only. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use access rules on our database, signed messages for wallet verification and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your devices, accounts and wallets.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as BagVerse develops. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the BagVerse team through our official channels.
